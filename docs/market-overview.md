# Market overview by region

Snapshot as of 2026-11-17. Figures and sources are in [`data/companies.csv`](../data/companies.csv).

## Europe: deep science, small rounds

- **Profile:** academic spinouts in Cambridge, Barcelona, Germany and the Netherlands, with seed and Series A rounds of roughly €12–50M.
- **Clinical:** Peptomyc OMO-103 (Phase 2) and MODAG anle138b (Phase 1b, licensed to Teva).
- **Platform companies:** Peptone (HDX-MS + AI, Evotec deal), Transition Bio (condensates, BMS among its investors, Voyager deal), Nuage (AR and transcription-factor condensates, Sofinnova).
- **New model:** Bind Research, the UK's first not-for-profit Focused Research Organisation (Feb 2025), funded to produce open IDP data.

## USA: the biggest money, the biggest corrections

- **Profile:** venture-created companies (Third Rock, Apple Tree, Flagship-style). It holds the largest rounds ever raised in the field: Dewpoint (>$287M), Faze ($81M) and Nereid ($50M).
- **2022–2025 correction:** Faze closed, ESSA's AR-NTD drug failed, Kronos wound down and Dewpoint cut about 70% of its staff.
- **2025–2026 reboot:** an AI-native wave. Topos Bio ($10.5M seed, ensemble foundation models), Aikium (designed binders) and Baker-lab IDR binder methods are open for spinouts. ARPA-H funds disordered-protein research at UC Berkeley.
- **Clinical:** Dewpoint's DPTX3186 is the first condensate modulator with an open IND and Fast Track designation.

## China: early stage, policy-backed capital

- **Profile:** phase-separation startups backed by Shanghai government-guided funds and leading VCs.
  - **抟相医药** (founded 2022, Pilong Li of Tsinghua) closed a Pre-A in Feb 2025 and is preparing INDs in autoimmunity and oncology.
  - **奕拓医药** (Shanghai) targets "undruggable" oncology targets, including through LLPS. It is backed by Hillhouse and an AstraZeneca–CICC fund and has collaborated with Roche China since 2021.
  - **途深智合** is an AI protein-design company that lists protein disordered regions among its target classes. It raised several hundred million RMB in Nov 2026.
- **Gaps:** English names and pipelines are often undisclosed. Large AI drug-discovery players (e.g. XtalPi, DP Technology) have no IDP programs we could verify. They are on the watchlist and not in the dataset.
